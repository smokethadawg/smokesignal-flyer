📤 Automated Outreach

Our outreach system automatically distributes missing pet flyers to local communities.

How Our Outreach Works

  • ✅ Automatic Trigger: Outreach begins immediately after flyer generation
  • ✅ Professional Flyers: Each outreach includes the generated PDF flyer with all pet details
  • ✅ Urgent Subject Lines: Emails use attention-grabbing subjects like "MISSING: [Pet Name] – [Breed] – [Location]"
  • ✅ Minimal Body Text: Messages keep text brief with the flyer containing all key information
  • ✅ Local Focus: Outreach targets communities in and around the pet's last known location
  • ✅ Multi-Channel: Distribution via email, social media, and local community groups

Example Outreach Email

Subject: URGENT - MISSING: Smoke – Blue/Grey Cane Corso – East Selah

A beloved pet has gone missing in your area.

Please see attached flyer for complete details and contact information.

If you have any information, please contact the owner immediately.

📎 Attachment: Missing Pet Flyer (PDF)

Outreach Status

When you submit a missing pet report, the system automatically:

  1. Generates a professional PDF flyer
  2. Saves the flyer to our database
  3. Triggers automated outreach to relevant channels
  4. Provides you with a downloadable copy
